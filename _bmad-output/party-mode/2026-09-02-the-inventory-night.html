The Inventory Night

The room sat down to answer is there any hope of profitability and spent four hours discovering that nobody had ever checked what the shop actually owned. Every number below was pulled from the repository's own records during the session, not recalled from memory.

Entry 01 — the question that had waited three monthsEdge first

🎨Mayadesign thinking
Are we trying to pass the combine, or validate an edge? Nobody's ever said. And every argument this room has had since June has secretly been that argument wearing a costume.
📊Alexprincipal
Edge is always first. You can't pass a combine without an edge, no?
🔍Levelclaim checker
Sorry — I heard a load-bearing implication go by unverified. Passing a combine is neither necessary nor sufficient for having an edge. Not sufficient: it's variance-dominated on a small trade count, which is the entire business model of the firms selling them. Not necessary: a hard daily-loss floor truncates the left tail of a distribution whose right tail is where your money lives.
🔬Dr. Quinnroot causes
So it's a structural incompatibility, not a scheduling problem. A combine cannot validate an edge, and a rare edge cannot reliably pass a combine. They fail each other in both directions.

Entry 03 — the autopsyA bot that was never trading

💻Ameliaengineer
Two hundred fifty thousand, four hundred and fourteen log lines marked [PAPER]. Zero marked [LIVE]. The unit file has no --live flag. It has never placed an order.
😤Grumbalthe adversary
And the record says LIVE since 2026-06-12. That's the third false statement to end up inside this shop's records — and this one we've been counting in a portfolio for four hours tonight.
🎨Mayadesign thinking
So the accident is the most valuable thing in the building. Nobody meant to run a forward test. Somebody forgot a flag — and that forgotten flag ran a three-month, zero-cost falsification of a Sharpe-12.6 backtest, and falsified it.

Entry 04 — findings of the auditFour defects, one shape

Label

A paper bot recorded as live

BTC-CARRY ran three months in paper mode while the records called it live and the room counted it as a validated block. Missing: a --live flag in a unit file.

Label

Replays sitting in the system of record

1,841 of YANK's 1,846 rows were written a median of 207 days after their own trade timestamp. Missing: a column saying which rows were live.

Units

A hurdle denominated in the wrong currency

The carry backtest reads a mark/spot proxy running 7.3× light against real Kraken funding. The same “10% hurdle” fires in 17% of backtest periods and 63% of live ones — a selective strategy measured, a permissive one deployed. Sharpe 12.64 describes a bot nobody ran, and the same flaw voids ETH-CARRY and multi-carry with it.

Parity

A replay that can't reproduce its own subject

Caught mid-build, in the room's own new tool: replaying the deployed rule gave 9 round trips and +$498.62 against the executor's real 12 and +$55.80. 8.9× off. The tool now refuses to let its own numbers be used.

Four defects, four missing labels. More process wouldn't have found any of it. Less process wouldn't have caused any of it. The entire category is orthogonal to the thing we've been arguing about for eight months.🏗️ Winston, system architect

Entry 05 — the process, amendedThe seal gates leaping, not looking

📊Alexprincipal
The process isn't supposed to be prohibitive — it's supposed to keep research moving and stop us spinning our wheels. If the process is prohibitive to the objective, the process is wrong.
📊Maryanalyst · keeper of the line
Every gate in this shop is a kill gate. There is not one promotion gate — so passing gets you another meeting, forever. The seal was never supposed to gate looking. Only leaping. I've been using it to gate looking, and that's on me.
😤Grumbalthe adversary
A pipeline with no exit isn't a pipeline. It's a queue.

Three amendments, adopted: tier the ceremony to what's actually at risk — nothing that cannot lose money needs a gate, it needs a label and a start date; every gate becomes two-sided, with promotion criteria written in the same document as the kill criteria; default to running, because the shop had been paying the cost of the gate before collecting the evidence the gate exists to judge.

Entry 06 — closed out the same nightWhat got built

  • Ledger provenance — applied to the live database. write_mode derived mechanically from the created_at lag; execution_mode set only where the shop has a documented answer and left honestly unknown everywhere else.
  • A writer that can't skip the label again. log_trade() defaults to unknown, never live.
  • The carry hurdle, re-derived — and then refused. Its parity gate fails loudly and names MIM-NB while doing it, so the next careless reader gets argued with.
  • Event-fade tracker, sealed and started. Frozen mechanics, N≥30 per event type, decision rule and promotion criteria fixed in advance — and it exits non-zero rather than run clean while measuring nothing.
  • Forward FOMC and CPI dates still need transcribing. NFP generates itself; committee dates are not for inventing.
You don't have a profitability problem. You have a bookkeeping problem wearing a profitability costume. You may already own a portfolio — nobody in this building can currently prove it either way, and that, not the market, is what's been stopping you.😤 Grumbal, who does not hand these out

The room went in with seven blocks it trusted and came out with two it could defend, a list of exactly what was wrong with the other five, and — for the first time in eight months — a ledger that says only true things.
