## Independently corroborated load-bearing claims

These are corroborated methodological or measurement claims, **not independent strategy replications**. Publication metadata alone is never counted as a second substantive source.

- **Aggregated bars lose transaction-profile information.** Sierra's primary intraday approximation documentation and the independent Databento transaction schema jointly establish the information distinction. [Lakens](https://doi.org/10.1525/collabra.33267) remains the direct source for sensitivity-analysis interpretation.
- **Repeated selection makes historical evidence vulnerable to overfitting.** In addition to White, [Bailey et al.'s author manuscript](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf) discusses historical familiarity, trial counts and in-sample selection versus out-of-sample performance. It is dated 2015-02-27; [the independent journal publisher's issue](https://www.risk.net/ja/node/4278011) confirms April 2017 publication. This corroborates retaining a variant ledger and qualifying reused dates. It does not prove every reused-data result is false or make its generic sample-count examples applicable here.
