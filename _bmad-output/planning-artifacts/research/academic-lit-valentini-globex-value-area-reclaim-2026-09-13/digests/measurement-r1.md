## Measurement decision

**Directly measurable:** one-minute close and total traded contract volume, plus traded volume at each price from complete transaction records. Construct developing volume profile H_t(p) = sum of trade sizes at price p from scheduled session open through the chosen observation cutoff; apply a specified contiguous POC-outward 70% algorithm. True profile requires transaction prices and quantities (M4, M7–M8); full MBO is unnecessary unless studying book behavior.

**Proxy:** distributing each minute's volume evenly over every 0.25-point level between its low and high. This conserves total volume but invents its price allocation. Mathematical counterexample: four trades at prices 100, 101, 100.25, 100.50 with sizes 1,1,97,1 and sizes 1,1,1,97 give identical OHLCV (O=100,H=101,L=100,C=100.50,V=100), but different price-volume concentration. The values are illustrative, not market observations. Thus OHLCV does not identify the transaction profile, even when bar volume is exact. No retrieved evidence bounds the resulting MNQ VAL error.

**Absorption is not identified:** falling bar volume followed by rising bar volume measures relative activity, not aggressive selling absorbed by replenishing passive buyers. OHLCV omits aggressor and resting-order fields present in M4–M6. Aggressor trades plus quotes/depth can support an operational absorption *proxy* (execution pressure, limited price response, replenishment); they do not establish trader intent. No causal or profitable-absorption claim is supported here.

## Required definitions before measurement

These are analyst design requirements inferred from information loss and time ordering, not empirically selected trading parameters:

- Decide whether close at minute t compares with VAL through t, or VAL through t−1. Both can be observed after close, but describe different events. Never replace historical developing VAL with final session VAL.
- Define the below-VAL episode, allowed reclaim delay, whether VAL remains frozen at excursion or develops, strict/inclusive boundary comparisons, and whether inside means between both VAL and VAH. A boundary moving below an unchanged close differs from price moving above a fixed boundary.
- Define falling/rising relative to the previous bar, a sequence, or another fixed reference; preserve equal-volume and empty-minute cases. Comparing immediately consecutive bars is measurable but has no retrieved evidence of being the intended Valentini specification.
- Signal is known after completed bar; execution simulation must begin after that availability time. Same-bar close execution needs a separately justified execution model.

## Data coverage and event hygiene

Use explicit MNQ expiry identities and full session-open history through each signal, including the preceding calendar evening. Maintain date-aware exchange sessions in America/Chicago or America/New_York and convert to UTC; use dated holiday/expiry/halt exceptions (M1–M3). A fixed UTC-midnight cut misses part of Globex; a UTC-day book snapshot cannot restore missing traded volume (M5–M6).

For volume, count economic executions once: prefer trades schema, or MBO T records; do not sum all MBO sizes or sum T and F together (M4–M6; CME-specific normalization follow-up pending). Do not deduplicate by timestamp alone or assume an order ID represents one trade. Keep source-file manifests and half-open request boundaries; test repeated-file overlap without collapsing distinct executions. Data-quality flags, missing starts, truncation, and timestamp semantics require explicit audits (M5).

An incomplete window is insufficient for full-session profile validation. Report its coverage and restrict any claim to that window; do not reset the profile at download start and call it Globex. Predeclare coverage eligibility without looking at favorable signals or outcomes. Excluding incomplete sessions conditions inference on available sessions; report eligible versus excluded counts and coverage by date/time. These are methodological deductions, not measured properties of a project dataset.
