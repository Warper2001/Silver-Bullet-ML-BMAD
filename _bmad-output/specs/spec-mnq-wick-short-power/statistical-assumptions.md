# Statistical assumptions

This is a one-sided normal approximation at alpha 0.05 and target power 0.80.
Cluster-robust standard errors use centered outcome sums divided by the number
of signals. Session and calendar-week clustering are competing dependence
proxies; the larger is selected per shift. Shift summaries are descriptive
sensitivity summaries, not a bootstrap confidence interval or evidence of a
strategy edge. NIST's sample-size discussion is the methodological reference:
https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc222.htm.
