# Kronos prospective evaluation design

`strategy_test_permitted=false`; `trading_authorized=false`.

This offline documentary command compares three, six and twelve calendar months of future contemporaneous shadow forecasts with concealed outcomes. It reads documentary inputs only, never forecasts, market bars, credentials or network endpoints. It always returns `PARK_PENDING_EVIDENCE` (exit 2); it implements no evidence-sufficiency admission assessment. Hashes establish identity, not truth or sufficiency.

```sh
PYTHONPATH=. /root/Silver-Bullet-ML-BMAD/.venv/bin/python -m research.kronos_readiness design \
  --source-pack docs/reports/kronos-design/sources-20260922/source-pack.json \
  --planning-start 2026-09-23 \
  --output-dir docs/reports/kronos-design/new-unused-run
```

The CLI requires an explicit `--planning-start`; the report also records the next full assumed RTH opening after generation in America/New_York: today before 09:30, otherwise the next weekday, excluding supplied holidays and early closes. This remains hypothetical until a product-specific calendar is reviewed. Explicit starts are hypothetical dates, not launch permission. Fresh output is required; aliases and data/market/cache/credential paths are refused. Pack and source files must be single-link regular files, each at most 8 MiB, read with a bounded read. FIFOs, hard links and market/market_data paths are refused. Explicit source-pack membership authorizes a documentary read; it does not prove content truth. Keep source documents and pack immutable during generation.

The strict JSON pack has the fields below, plus optional `strategy_test_permitted` and `trading_authorized` only when both are exactly false:

- `schema_version`: integer `1`.
- `sources`: list of objects with exactly `id`, `path`, `sha256`, `date`, `url`, `claim`. IDs are unique letters/digits/hyphens/underscores; paths are absolute or relative to the pack; dates are `YYYY-MM-DD`. URL is documentary provenance only and is never fetched. All source bytes must match SHA-256. Missing indispensable evidence is represented by absent sources and remains a blocker; a claimed source with missing bytes or a wrong hash refuses execution.
- `calendar`: null or an object with `coverage_start`, `coverage_end` (inclusive ISO dates), nonempty `source_ids`, `closed_dates`, `early_close_dates`. Dates must be unique and within coverage. These are source assertions; incomplete coverage falls back to weekday assumptions on uncovered dates. Even full proxy coverage cannot establish an authenticated MNQ calendar.
- `scenarios`: nonempty list with unique `name`, explicit ISO-date lists `roll_dates`, `outage_dates`, `incomplete_dates`, and a nonnegative integer `calibration_sessions`. All dates are assumptions, not selected favorable sessions. Overlapping loss causes are retained but deducted only once. Dates outside a horizon do not affect that horizon.
- `economic`: null, or `k_effect`, `incremental_effect`, `k_variance`, `m_variance`, `covariance`, `se_inflation`, `independent_evidence`. The evidence object maps those five economic inputs and `dependence` to existing source IDs. References do not certify independence. Effects must be independently chosen before sample-size selection; another strategy's moments are inadmissible.

No operational, authentication or market-data arguments are accepted. Generated JSON includes both false permission flags. Exact documentary copies are stored inside flagged JSON envelopes as base64 bytes with original hashes; decoding produces the original source. `COMPLETE.json` hashes every generated artifact, plus implementation and frozen inputs. `comparison.md` shows conditional session capacity and standardized sensitivities; `scenarios.json` retains the complete session ledger and deductions; `evidence.json` preserves claims and provenance; `protocol.json` describes future prerequisites.

The calendar model assumes 26 completed 15-minute bars per full RTH and 14 on an early close. It starts with zero context, resets on each supplied contract roll, and needs 128 completed same-contract bars plus room for the unchanged four-bar forecast horizon. Hence the fifth full day can finish warmup but cannot offer a four-bar forecast; the sixth can. A fully warmed early close can be eligible. Holiday closures contribute no bars. Outages and incomplete sessions contribute no bars and conservatively reset context in this scenario model; this is an additional planning assumption, not a modification to the frozen replay engine. Every weekday has a retained accounting record, including exclusions. Warmup deductions separately attribute initial, contract-reset and gap-reset losses without adding these subtotals twice.

Collection capacity and untouched evaluation N are separate. The requested calibration reservation is removed from conditional collection capacity. Zero assumes calibration was independently completed before the horizon; positive allocation is only hypothetical resource allocation and does not establish powered calibration. The ledger assigns chronological hypothetical calibration and evaluation roles and reports their boundary dates. Calibration analysis and the separate preregistration freeze must finish before the first evaluation cutoff; allocation alone cannot establish this, and delay requires recalculating the timeline and power. Separate preregistrations and power gates are required before performance calibration and evaluation. Seeds, arms and overlapping forecasts do not increase the sampling unit of one eligible session.

Power uses the existing paired normal known-variance calculation: two one-sided tests, alpha .025 each, .90 marginal targets, joint lower bound .80. Paired variance is Var(K)+Var(M)-2Cov(K,M). Missing independent economics yields standardized detectable effects at hypothetical SE inflations 1, 1.5 and 2; neither these assumptions nor conditional power success permits a test. Calendar, economics, dependence, operational timing/coverage, costs, provenance and population controls require independent review before reconsideration.

Future collection and shadow forecasting require separate admission. Proposed endpoint paths, cadence, retrieval depth, request ceiling, quote fields/checks, clock controls and recovery are documented in `protocol.json`; they are unvalidated proposals. Dedicated research authentication must be isolated from shared trading credentials and refresh state.

At each future decision cutoff, freeze hashes of explicitly completed observations actually received by then. Revisions received later affect later decisions only. Record actual forecast start/completion and input availability. Only a minute-open strictly after conservative availability may fill; overlapping uncertainty intervals block causal ordering. Recovered observations have new receipt times and cannot recreate missed contemporaneous forecasts. Keep scheduled flatten/reset unchanged; preserve missing flatten prices, outages, incomplete sessions and unresolved exposure without invented exits or selective deletion.

Integrity reports must reveal no forecasts, targets, positions or outcomes. Use a separately access-controlled archive, log human/tool exposure, and quarantine the affected population before any evaluation continues. Prospective actual receipt/completion rules take precedence over the nested frozen candidate modeled-latency field, which is historical replay metadata only. Synthetic ordering checks normalize times to UTC, including DST folds, and assume an uninterrupted schedule of every whole UTC minute before the supplied scheduled flatten. They require the first eligible minute open and reject completion/fill at or after flatten; a missing real open requires a separately admitted schedule. These checks test invariants only; they are not a production ingestion, replay or eligibility system.
