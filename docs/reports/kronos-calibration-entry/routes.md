# Three routes through the calibration dependency

The governing policy requires a power gate before any new strategy test. The existing prospective design explicitly requires separate gates before performance calibration and evaluation (`inputs/01-kronos-design.md`). The current seal admits neither. Statistical planning choices below are unadopted proposals; they do not revise that policy.

The frozen candidate is one MNQ, 128 completed same-contract 15-minute bars, four-bar forecast horizon, sign of the mean of three terminal closes versus observed close, with the existing four-bar momentum and flat arms. Seeds remain one ensemble. Strict-after-availability fills, scheduled flatten, reset and incomplete-outcome handling are preserved. Exact candidate identities are snapshotted in `inputs/08-11-candidate-protocol.json`; the seal's corrected mean rule governs, not its superseded transcription of “unanimous.”

**A — literature applicability.** Shi et al.'s Kronos paper uses a daily stock portfolio investment simulation (CSI indices, ranking/turnover rules and a multiday horizon), not this one-contract MNQ intraday strategy or paired momentum comparison. Its published results do not supply the required dollar effects, joint moments or execution model. This is a bounded-search finding, not proof that qualifying evidence exists nowhere. [Primary paper, investment simulation appendix](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.02739v1).

**B — what the XSMOM firewall does and does not show.** `inputs/12-xsmom1_power_gate.py` was read, never imported or executed. It computes rank-IC under shifted/bootstrap pairings and refuses the entire identity vector. It is tied to its own seal, cross-sectional ranks, frozen effect conversion and weekly block assumptions. None of its estimates or constants is transferred.

The identity-vector rejection is weaker than banning every aligned observation: a nonidentity permutation can contain fixed points, and bootstrap blocks can overlap original indices. Its circular-shift exclusion is a different, stronger local constraint. This is a code-inspection caveat, not a new audit verdict on XSMOM. A future Kronos firewall needs information-flow review of every intermediate, diagnostic and output; “do not print the mean” is insufficient.

For example, suppose iid session pairs have `K=Z`, `M=-Z`, `Var(Z)=1`. Actual paired variance is 4. Independently mismatching M makes covariance zero and variance 2, understating required N by a factor of two. With `M=K`, true paired variance is zero but mismatching produces variance 2. This establishes neither direction of conservatism in general. Common shifts of both arms preserve same-session pairing but may retain the aligned performance itself; they do not automatically provide concealment. Price volatility is not a sufficient proxy for strategy PnL risk when timing, positions, costs and turnover depend on the signal.

If defensible marginal SD upper bounds exist, Cauchy–Schwarz gives `Var(K-M) <= (sigma_K+sigma_M)^2`, regardless of same-session correlation. It does not bound serial dependence or provide those SD bounds. A joint region for long-run covariance matrices is preferable. Without such evidence, setting covariance to zero, fixing block length, or assuming an SE inflation of two is not conservative by definition.

**C — precision design without an exemption.** Whitehead et al. study external pilot variance planning and uncertainty inflation under specified trial assumptions, with separate pilot/main populations. Their work supports accounting for pilot uncertainty; clinical sample-size rules and iid normal assumptions are not evidence for trading. See [the primary paper, sections 2.1–2.2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4876429/). No numeric pilot size is imported from its recommendations.

Under iid normal paired differences, `nu=n_cal-1` and `nu*s_D^2/sigma_D^2 ~ chi-square(nu)`. An upper bound is `U_D=nu*s_D^2/chi2(gamma,nu)` with coverage `1-gamma`. Relative CI width can be planned from chi-square quantiles without knowing the variance scale. This can remove one mathematical unknown for a *precision* objective. It does not establish normality, independent session count, policy admission, a useful effect, or an available precision budget. A paired pilot has `n_cal-1` degrees of freedom, not the parallel-arm trial formula `2*n_cal-2`.

Before this route could proceed, a future proposal must justify why its specific power/precision admission test satisfies the existing policy, or seek an explicit policy decision outside this task. If a supported route A or B first supplies a conservative variance/dependence envelope, it may permit a gate before performance calibration; otherwise requiring the calibration's own measured variance to admit it is circular. The present decision does not ask for a policy waiver as a substitute for evidence.

**Future containment requirements, common to all routes.** Preregister distinct calibration and evaluation populations and a finite calendar/cash/operator-time cap. Freeze nuisance summaries, costs, economic alternatives, missingness rules, statistical procedure and candidate before evaluation begins. Calibration observations never migrate into evaluation. No optional extension until results become favorable. A larger required N at the resource cap yields UNDERPOWERED or PARK, not a revised useful effect.

Retain scheduled sessions and reasons for incompleteness in an integrity ledger. Missing exits and unresolved exposure are unknown outcomes, not zero PnL. Do not discard losing/incomplete sessions or treat a last mark as an executed flatten. Until a preregistered, justified missingness or partial-identification method resolves the estimand, calibration cannot claim variance/precision and evaluation cannot start; unresolved exposure blocks a clean population/contract reset. With no credible outcome bounds, partial-identification intervals may be unbounded. Recovery does not recreate contemporaneous input availability or forecasts.

An accidental disclosure includes forecasts, targets, positions, outcomes or diagnostics from which they can be reconstructed. Record scope, time, personnel/tools and affected sessions; stop access, quarantine affected populations, audit contamination and revise resource accounting. Do not relabel exposed sessions as untouched, expand collection automatically, or restart under an unchanged evidential claim. Blinded integrity reporting must itself be checked for leakage.
