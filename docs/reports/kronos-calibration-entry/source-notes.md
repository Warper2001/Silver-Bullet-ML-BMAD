Local documents are evidence of repository state and requirements, not evidence that a market strategy works. `input-register.json` records exact hashes and original paths, anchored to starting commit `c4e81e01474ff6f85ec8f7e7f60498efbe19809d`. Its thirteen snapshots contain policy, design/readiness/replay documentation, the prior horizon comparison/report/scenario ledger/manifest, candidate protocol, conditional power, provider findings, the readiness preregistration, and the XSMOM gate source. The gate source is never executed. Original source references are retained in copied documents.

Search scope was bounded to existing Kronos documentary artifacts and primary official pricing/methodological sources. It did not inspect private account records, market files, the sealed holdout, saved forecast paths, strategy outcome ledgers, or credentials. The paper's investment-design section was read to assess applicability, not used as a sample of strategy results. A claim that no qualifying independent evidence was *identified here* is not a universal nonexistence claim.

Evidence classification: unit conversions and public fee schedules are supported documentary inputs; statistical identities are derivations checked synthetically; inherited capacities are conditional assumptions; account economics, strategy-specific nuisance parameters and precision-model applicability are unresolved. No numerical performance input is adopted. Numeric alpha/power choices come from the existing design; synthetic moments and confidence examples are visibly marked and cannot satisfy an admission gate.
