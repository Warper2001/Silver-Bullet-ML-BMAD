## Calibration design, draft only

This is a **design proposal, not a committed experiment registration**. It authorizes no strategy outcome calculation or parameter change. The completed power gate supplies the current verdict; it is not relabeled POWERED to enable this study.

1. **Fix data roles.** Retain the original May pilot as measurement/development evidence. Assign the entire proposed historical quarter to calibration/development, acknowledging prior exposure to 2025 data elsewhere in the project. It cannot be called untouched validation. Choose future confirmation dates only after implementation, nuisance estimates, costs and the test design are frozen; do not inspect those outcomes in the meantime. One contract quarter is not proof of representative future regimes.
2. **Audit measurement first.** Preserve provider receipts and raw hashes; reconcile instrument definitions, feed/status events, event completion, trade counts and volume. Source dated July 4 and Labor Day schedules and session-reset behavior. CME [removed the afternoon pause in 2021](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/electronic-trading/2021/06/20210621.html). Missing data must remain distinct from zero-trade intervals. Report every excluded candidate and its outcome-independent reason; do not turn exclusions into trading filters.
3. **Measure costs without adopting a favorable setting.** Describe spread, displayed depth and quote availability across complete candidate sessions before selecting any execution model. Keep capture timestamps distinct from our receipt and routing times. Topstep's current, product-specific [MNQ fee table](https://help.topstep.com/en/articles/8284213-topstepx-commissions-and-fees) lists **$1.22 per contract round trip** as of July 28, 2026. Treat that as a prospective fee scenario, verify the operator's actual account charges, and label it explicitly if applied to 2025 prices. It is not a historical 2025 fee estimate and excludes spread/slippage and fixed account costs. No new live cost constant is set.
4. **Register a descriptive nuisance study before outcomes.** A subsequent registration must explicitly permit fixed-rule calibration calculations and specify the execution/cost uncertainty treatment. Keep the approved profile, volume comparisons, next-minute entry, excursion stop and VAH target unchanged. Retain full-session one-contract outcomes including zero-trade sessions. Estimate variability and temporal dependence with uncertainty; report failures and unstable estimates. This descriptive stage does not claim positive expectancy or approve deployment. Do not select a favorable effect from its mean, choose a calendar filter, or tune the strategy from these outcomes.
5. **Recalculate power for a separately frozen test.** Establish an economically justified minimum worthwhile net effect independently of favorable validation results. Combine that target with calibrated variability, dependence and cost uncertainty to calculate an achievable validation horizon. Keep the result undetermined if those inputs remain unsupported. Fix the confirmatory test and stopping rule before its outcomes. A second block or prospective collection may be needed; no future sample count or dollar threshold is invented here.
