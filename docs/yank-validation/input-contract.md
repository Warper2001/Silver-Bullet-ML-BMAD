## Parsing and availability

_parse_bar parses TimeStamp using ISO conversion (Z -> UTC offset), floats OHLC, integer TotalVolume (missing -> zero), and synthesizes positive notional only for DollarBar validation. Parse failures are skipped. Completion/status fields are ignored. Poll receipt clock is datetime.now(UTC); label <= clock is sufficient, without an end-of-minute check. Raw response order is preserved. After each admitted row the watermark immediately advances: duplicate and revised timestamps <= watermark are discarded, including out-of-order earlier rows in that same response. Future labels are skipped; delayed rows with labels above watermark are accepted and subjected to stale detection. This is baseline behavior, not evidence of provider guarantees. End/start labeling, server ordering, update cadence, partial-row flags, late revisions and receipt latency remain unverified TradeStation semantics. Offline rows must retain request identity, raw response order, receipt timestamp and completion evidence; native finalized minute OHLC cannot establish the live receipt path.

## Session, contract and warm-up

The buffer caps at 7,500 accepted bars. Session statistics reset by America/New_York calendar date (not exchange session); session open is first accepted bar at/after 06:00 ET. Daily range history caps at 20. DST uses timezone rules. Symbols are explicit quarterly contracts; there is no automatic YANK roll or continuous-series adjustment. Missing minutes are not filled. H1 resamples accepted rows, excludes final bucket; at least 60 M1 rows and two H1 buckets are needed to begin. Sweep requires lookback+5 completed H1 buckets; volatility has its own ATR/history requirements. M15 uses last 3,000 rows, drops empty buckets, excludes final bucket, requires seven completed buckets, and latches until sweep reset. Partial or missing buckets are not guaranteed full calendar coverage. LR passes through below 1,950 rows. Admission must separately establish full warm-up (120 H1 volatility history plus ATR prehistory, LR history and session features); merely counting rows is insufficient.
