# Frozen-baseline diagnostic definitions

All history is exposed development evidence. This package describes existing recorded executions; it performs no simulation, parameter test, filter selection or efficacy analysis. No power gate applies to descriptive accounting. Every future strategy test requires its own suitable power gate and committed preregistration, untouched development-independent data and a falsifier; ambiguous evidence is a FAIL.

Primary: arm A, delay 2, one MNQ at $2 per index point, $2.24 roundtrip. Delay 2 means the open one full minute after the decision. A source event label is the completed-minute label, one minute after a bar-open modeled fill; both timestamps remain in the event ledger. Alternatives in scenarios.csv were already recorded in the source; they are timing/cost sensitivity displays, never new benchmarks.

Source row order is execution order within each minute. A position switch across zero is a true reversal with two $1.12 legs. A recorded REVERSAL filled after a protective exit can be an opposite entry after flat; original labels remain unchanged. Costs occur once at each recorded leg, including entry costs of open positions. Minute-close marked net equity equals realized gross plus unrealized gross minus fees incurred so far. No reserve for a future exit fee is deducted. Flat eligible days contribute 390 zero-change minute marks and remain in daily/drawdown grids.

Thirteen fixed cash-session half-hours begin 09:30 ET. Completed labels 09:31 through 10:00 belong to 09:30–10:00, and similarly through 16:00. Entry cohorts use entry event labels; accrual buckets use labels of the minute in which marked P&L changes, including execution costs. These clocks are different questions. America/New_York is used through DST; modeled timestamps retain explicit offsets. Attribution dimensions are direction, original exit label, calendar year, first/subsequent entry in session and entry half-hour. Counts for accrual count entry executions, so every partition sums to 801; they are not counts of all trades touching each bucket. Losing-trade dollars are positive absolute losses of final losing trades. For accrual, eventual_losers_net_contribution is the actual signed minute net change of trades whose final net is negative. The partition losing_trade_dollars column negates this signed contribution, so favorable periods can have negative loss dollars and negative shares; it is not a final-loss amount allocated to time. Shares divide by total losing-trade dollars, not net profit.

Gross accrual follows each held trade's successive close marks and terminal recorded fill. An exit at the next bar open books its gap/change in that bar's completed-minute bucket. An uncertain CAT_STOP uses its recorded fill with interval [event minus one minute, event]; no exact stop timestamp is invented. Prices after an exit cannot affect that trade. Sampled excursions include entry/exit fills and completed held closes. Fully held minute high/low ranges provide additional observed extrema. Stop-minute high/low/close are excluded, even on same-minute entry/stop or adverse gaps. Stop extrema are incomplete observed lower bounds, with duration bounded by the event interval. Exposure upper counts any held minute including the uncertain stop minute; lower excludes that minute. An opening exit occupies zero of its terminal minute. Upper exposure matches the source's historical contract-minute convention.

Drawdown is sampled at completed minute marks or session closes from initial zero P&L, with prior sessions carried forward. Depth is dollars below the running maximum; sample durations count observations, not uninterrupted clock minutes. Overnight, weekend and holiday elapsed time is not exposure. No claim about unobserved intraminute portfolio extrema is made. The best 67 sessions and top 5% of trades are hindsight concentration descriptions, never removal rules or future selection criteria.

Outputs are sealed only after source/data bindings validate. Data are SHA256 bound; compact input evidence, source files, definitions and versions are snapshotted. Runs refuse overwrites and preserve failure.json plus a completion inventory on errors. Verification checks required inventory independently of listed hashes and recomputes accounting from exported fills and joins. Provenance status is observational, with collection timestamps and explicit missing evidence; current official calendar support does not establish original point-in-time availability.
